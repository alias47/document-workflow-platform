# Product Vision

**Project Name:** TBD (Working Name: Document Workflow Platform)

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

---

# Vision Statement

To become the most flexible and user-friendly platform for collecting, managing, reviewing, and tracking client documents through customizable workflows.

The platform should eliminate fragmented document management processes by providing organizations with a secure, centralized workspace while giving applicants a simple and transparent experience.

---

# Mission

Our mission is to replace inefficient document collection methods such as WhatsApp, email, Google Drive, spreadsheets, and paper-based processes with a modern cloud platform that simplifies collaboration between organizations and their applicants.

We aim to reduce manual work, improve transparency, and provide a better experience for both organizations and their clients.

---

# The Problem

Organizations that work with clients often require dozens of documents throughout different stages of a process.

Today this is usually managed using:

* WhatsApp
* Email
* Google Drive
* Excel
* Paper files

This creates several problems:

* Documents are scattered across multiple platforms.
* Staff spend significant time requesting missing documents.
* There is no clear approval process.
* Applicants cannot easily track their progress.
* Organizations have no centralized audit history.
* Communication becomes difficult as the number of applicants grows.

These problems increase operational costs and reduce customer satisfaction.

---

# Our Solution

The platform provides a centralized workspace where organizations can:

* Register applicants.
* Request required documents.
* Review uploaded documents.
* Approve or reject submissions.
* Track applicant progress.
* Maintain a complete activity history.
* Collaborate internally through notes and workflow updates.

Applicants receive a dedicated portal where they can:

* View required documents.
* Upload files securely.
* Replace rejected documents.
* Track approval status.
* Monitor overall progress.

---

# Target Market

The platform is designed to support any organization that collects and reviews documents from applicants.

Examples include:

* Education Consultancies
* Recruitment Agencies
* Immigration Consultants
* HR Agencies
* Insurance Companies
* Financial Institutions
* Loan Providers
* Healthcare Providers
* Government Services
* Legal Firms

The first pilot customer is expected to be an education consultancy.

However, the platform architecture must remain industry-independent.

---

# Product Philosophy

The platform should be:

* Simple to use.
* Secure by default.
* Easy to configure.
* Mobile-ready.
* Scalable.
* Maintainable.
* Fast.
* Reliable.

Every feature should solve a real operational problem.

Complexity should only be introduced when supported by customer feedback.

---

# Core Product Principles

## 1. Simplicity First

The interface should be intuitive enough that new users require little or no training.

Every screen should have a clear purpose.

---

## 2. Single Source of Truth

Every applicant, document, workflow stage, and activity should exist in one centralized system.

Users should never need to search through multiple communication channels to find information.

---

## 3. Transparency

Applicants should always know:

* What documents are required.
* Which documents have been approved.
* Which documents require action.
* What stage they are currently in.

Organizations should always know:

* Applicant progress.
* Missing documents.
* Review history.
* Outstanding tasks.

---

## 4. Security

Documents are sensitive.

The platform must prioritize:

* Secure authentication.
* Secure file storage.
* Role-based permissions.
* Activity logging.
* Data privacy.

---

## 5. Scalability

Although the MVP serves a single organization, the architecture should support future growth into a multi-tenant SaaS platform without requiring major redesign.

# Target Users

## Organization Administrator

Responsible for:

* Managing the organization.
* Managing staff accounts and roles.
* Configuring organization settings.
* Monitoring operations.
* Accessing reports and analytics.
* Managing subscription and billing (Future).

---

## Staff (Consultant / Recruiter / HR)

Responsible for:

* Creating applicant profiles.
* Creating applicant portal accounts.
* Managing applicants throughout the workflow.
* Reviewing uploaded documents.
* Approving or rejecting documents.
* Updating workflow stages.
* Adding internal notes.
* Requesting additional documents.
* Communicating with applicants.

---

## Applicant (Portal User)

Applicants are invited to the platform by the organization.

Applicants do not self-register. Their portal account is created by a staff member during the onboarding process.

Responsible for:

* Logging into the portal using credentials provided by the organization.
* Changing their password on first login.
* Uploading required documents.
* Replacing rejected documents.
* Tracking application progress.
* Viewing document status.
* Viewing timeline and activity history.
* Updating limited profile information.

# Value Proposition

## For Organizations

* Centralized applicant and document management.
* Standardized onboarding workflow.
* Reduced administrative workload.
* Improved collaboration between staff.
* Faster document collection and review.
* Improved visibility into applicant progress.
* Secure storage of sensitive documents.

---

* Clear workflow management.
* Centralized communication history.

---

## For Applicants

* No registration process required.
* Organization-created portal account.
* Secure access to personal documents.
* Simple document upload experience.
* Real-time application progress tracking.
* Clear visibility of missing or rejected documents.

---

# Competitive Advantages

The platform differentiates itself by focusing on:

* Modern user experience.
* Simple onboarding.
* Secure document management.
* Applicant transparency.
* Configurable architecture.
* Future-ready white-label capabilities.

Rather than attempting to solve every business problem, the platform focuses on becoming the best solution for document collection and workflow management.

---

# Success Metrics

The pilot will be considered successful if:

* Organizations actively use the platform every day.
* Applicants complete document submission without assistance.
* Document review time decreases.
* Staff reduce dependence on external communication tools.
* Users report an improved experience compared to existing processes.

---

# Future Vision

The long-term objective is to evolve the platform into a fully configurable, white-label SaaS solution.

Organizations will eventually be able to:

* Create custom workflows.
* Configure required document types.
* Customize branding.
* Use custom domains.
* Integrate with third-party systems.
* Access mobile applications.
* Automate repetitive processes.
* Gain insights through analytics and reporting.

---

# Guiding Principle

> Build software that simplifies document-driven operations through clarity, security, and flexibility.

Every new feature should answer one question:

**"Does this make the document workflow simpler, faster, or more transparent for both the organization and the applicant?"**

If the answer is no, the feature should be reconsidered or postponed.
